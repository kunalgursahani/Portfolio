ABOUT

Ten years in, still building on the same principle.

I'm Kunal Gursahani, an SAP full-stack developer with over a decade spent inside the SAP ecosystem — from classic ABAP development to the RESTful ABAP Programming model, Fiori front ends, and the automation and AI layers now sitting on top of S/4HANA.

My work follows one rule: the core stays clean. Every extension — whether it's a RAP-based business object, a Fiori Elements app, an n8n workflow, or a Joule-powered assistant — is built to sit alongside SAP, not inside it. That's what keeps upgrades painless and systems ready for whatever SAP ships next.
